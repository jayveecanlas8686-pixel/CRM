Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
How do I sign in?
Navigate to the login page and enter your email and password. Use the demo accounts for testing: admin@example.com, manager@example.com, or agent@example.com — all with password123.
How do I add my first customer?
Go to Customers → click "Add Customer" → fill in the company name, contact person, and email → click Save. The customer will appear in your customer list.
What are the different user roles?
Admin: full access. Sales Manager: manage team, customers, leads, deals. Sales Agent: manage own customers and leads. Viewer: read-only access.
How do I reset the demo data?
Go to Settings → Data Management → click "Reset to Demo Data". This restores all sample records.
Customers
How do I view customer details?
Click the eye icon in the customer list, or navigate to customer-details.html?id=X. You'll see their full profile, deals, tasks, notes, and activities.
Can I link a deal to a customer?
Yes! When creating or editing a deal, select the customer from the dropdown. It will appear in the customer's "Deals" tab.
How do I add notes to a customer?
Open the customer details page, click the "Notes" tab, type your note, and click "Add Note". Notes are timestamped and attributed to your user.
Leads
What is a lead score?
Lead score (0-100) represents how qualified or likely to convert a lead is. Higher score = hotter lead. You can manually set this when adding/editing a lead.
How do I convert a lead to a customer?
In the leads list, click the green user-check icon on any active lead. Confirm the conversion and a new customer record will be created automatically.
What lead sources are available?
Website, LinkedIn, Referral, Cold Call, Event, Email Campaign, Social Media, and Other.
Pipeline
How does the Kanban pipeline work?
Deals are organized in columns by stage. Drag a deal card to a different column to move it. The probability updates automatically based on the stage.
Can I customize pipeline stages?
Yes! Go to Settings → Pipeline Stages to rename stages, adjust probabilities, and change colors.
What is weighted revenue forecast?
It's the deal value multiplied by its probability percentage. E.g., a $100K deal at 60% probability = $60K weighted forecast.
Automation
How do I create an automation rule?
Go to Automation → click "Create Rule" → select a trigger event and value → choose an action and template → enable the rule → save.
When do automations run?
Automations fire when the trigger condition is met during normal CRM operations (e.g., changing a lead status, moving a deal stage). You can also run a manual simulation.
What actions can automations perform?
Create follow-up tasks, log activity notes, update task priority, or trigger email notifications.
Reports
How do I export a report?
In Reports, click any "Export CSV" button to download that report as a spreadsheet-compatible CSV file.
How is the revenue forecast calculated?
For each active deal: value × probability %. These are summed to give the weighted pipeline forecast.
Can I filter reports by date?
Yes! Use the date range pickers at the top of the Reports page and click Apply to filter the data.
